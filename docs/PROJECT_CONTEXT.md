# HOMECARE HEALTHCARE PLATFORM
# AI PROJECT CONTEXT & PRODUCT REFERENCE

## 1. PROJECT IDENTITY

This project is a modern digital platform for a professional home healthcare / homecare hospital organization.

The platform allows patients and their family members to discover, understand, enquire about, and request healthcare services delivered at home.

The project is inspired by the information architecture and service breadth of established home healthcare organizations such as Apollo HomeCare, but this project MUST NOT copy their branding, UI, visual identity, source code, text, images, or proprietary design.

The final product must have its own original brand identity, interface, content presentation, components, layouts, and user experience.

The reference website is used only to understand the type of healthcare services and information that a professional homecare organization may provide.

---

# 2. PRIMARY PRODUCT OBJECTIVE

The primary objective is:

"Make professional healthcare easier to discover and access from home."

The website must help a visitor quickly understand:

1. What healthcare services are available?
2. Which service is appropriate for their requirement?
3. Is the service available in their location?
4. How does the service work?
5. Why should they trust the organization?
6. How can they request/book the service?

The website should NOT behave like a simple informational hospital website.

It should behave like a modern healthcare service platform.

---

# 18. CONTENT PRINCIPLES

Website language should be:

Professional
Clear
Compassionate
Reassuring

Avoid unnecessary medical jargon.

When medical terminology is required, explain it in simple language.

Example:

BAD:

"Post-operative rehabilitation interventions facilitate optimized functional recovery."

BETTER:

"Recover comfortably at home with personalized rehabilitation support from trained professionals."

The final wording must be reviewed and approved by the organization.

---

# 19. MEDICAL SAFETY

This platform is a healthcare service platform.

The website must NOT:

- Diagnose users
- Guarantee medical outcomes
- Recommend prescription medication
- Replace emergency medical services
- Make unsupported medical claims
- Present AI output as medical advice
- Claim a treatment will definitely work

Where appropriate, display:

"For medical emergencies, contact your local emergency service or visit the nearest emergency department."

Exact emergency wording should be approved by the organization and adapted to its operating region.

# 32. MOST IMPORTANT AI INSTRUCTION

Treat this document as the project's foundational context.

Before implementing any feature, understand:

1. Who the user is.
2. What problem the feature solves.
3. Where the feature belongs in the overall journey.
4. Whether the feature improves healthcare discovery or access.
5. Whether it maintains trust and accessibility.
6. Whether it is safe and medically responsible.
7. Whether it matches the project's design system.
8. Whether it is reusable and scalable.

When there is a conflict between visual complexity and usability:

CHOOSE USABILITY.

When there is a conflict between conversion and healthcare trust:

CHOOSE TRUST.

When there is a conflict between speed and unnecessary features:

CHOOSE PERFORMANCE.

When there is a conflict between copying a reference website and creating an original product:

CHOOSE ORIGINAL DESIGN.
